Gehu Ki Katai — Gaon Ki Ek Subah

By Yugal

Kuch cheezon ka description dena mushkil hota hai. Gehu ki katai ka waqt kuch aisa hi hai mere liye. Jab April ka mahina aata hai aur hawa mein ek alag hi khusboo ghulne lagti hai — gehu ke peele khet, mitti ki sugandh, aur subah ki thandi hawa — tab lagta hai ki yeh waqt kisi bhi cheez se zyada real hai.

Is saal bhi wahi hua. Subah ki neend toot ti hai suraj nikalne se pehle. Ghar mein ek alag hi energy hoti hai un dinon — maa roti bana rahi hoti hai, chacha aur pitaji khet ki taraf nikalne ki tayyari mein hote hain, aur hum sab milke ek kaam mein lag jaate hain. Yeh koi ordinary din nahi hota — yeh harvest ka din hota hai.

Khet mein pohanche toh nazara hi alag tha. Puri zameen sone ki tarah chamak rahi thi. Gehu ki baaliyan sar jhuka ke khadi thi, jaise poori saal ki mehnat ka bojh utha rahi hon. Haath mein danti uthate hi ek purani feeling waapas aa jaati hai — yeh feeling kisi school ya college mein nahi milti, yeh sirf apni zameen par milti hai.

Katai ka kaam mushkil hai, koi shak nahi. Dhoop mein ghanton kaam karna, haath toot te hain, pith dard karti hai. Lekin jab sham ko sab thak ke baith jaate hain aur ek doosre ki taraf dekhte hain — toh ek aisi khushi hoti hai jo words mein explain nahi hoti. Kaam khatam hua, fasal aayi, sab theek hai.

Sheher mein rehne wale log bohot kuch miss karte hain. Woh miss karte hain woh subah jo sirf khet mein milti hai. Woh miss karte hain maa ke haath ki roti jo khet ke beech mein khaate hain. Woh miss karte hain us thakaan ko, jo neend se zyada sukoon deti hai.

Yeh story kisi bade kaam ki nahi — na koi moral hai, na koi lesson. Bas ek lamha hai jo yaad rakhna chahta hoon. Apne gaon ka, apni zameen ka, apne logon ka.
